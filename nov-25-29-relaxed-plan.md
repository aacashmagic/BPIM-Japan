# 25–29 Nov · Slow & relaxed: Haneda → Tokyo

**Land:** Wed 25 Nov, 15:30, Haneda · **Be in Tokyo by:** Sun 29 Nov evening
**Idea:** short hops (never more than ~1.5h), lots of chill time, see 3 places.

```
Haneda ──20 min──▶ YOKOHAMA (1 night) ──25 min──▶ KAMAKURA / ENOSHIMA (2 nights, beach)
       ──1.5h──▶ HAKONE (1 night, onsen) ──1.5–2h──▶ TOKYO
```

**Weather:** about 12–17 °C in the day. The beach is for chilling, not swimming. **The sun sets around 16:30.** Clear skies and Mt. Fuji views are common at this time of year.
**Rate:** ¥1 ≈ ₹0.61

---

## Before leaving Haneda
- **Send your big suitcase to your Tokyo address for the 29th** from the luggage-delivery counter in arrivals
  (~¥2,500–3,500 · ₹1,500–2,100). Travel with just a backpack. Check that your Tokyo place accepts deliveries.
- Add **Suica** to your phone (it works for every leg below) and get cash at the 7-Eleven ATM.

## Day by day

### Wed 25 · Yokohama: easy first night
- Land 15:30. **Keikyu line straight to Yokohama** (~20–30 min, ~¥370 · ₹230).
- Check in near **Sakuragicho or Kannai**.
- Evening:
  - Stroll the **Minato Mirai** waterfront night view.
  - The **Red Brick Warehouse** Christmas market usually starts around late November; check the dates.
  - Dinner in **Chinatown**, Japan's biggest.
  - Beer at a harbour-side craft bar. Yokohama is home to Kirin and a lot of craft breweries.

### Thu 26 · Slow morning in Yokohama → Kamakura
- Sleep in.
- **Cup Noodles Museum:** design and make your own cup noodle, ~¥500 + ¥500 (₹610 total). It's fun and very Japanese.
- Walk **Yamashita Park** by the sea.
- Early afternoon: **JR to Kamakura** (~25 min, ~¥360 · ₹220). Check in and drop your bag.
- **Sunset on Yuigahama beach**, then a relaxed dinner and drinks in town.

### Fri 27 · Beach day: do nothing (properly)
- Ride the **Enoden**, a retro train along the coast (day pass ~¥800 · ₹490), to **Enoshima island**.
- Explore the caves and shrines, eat a seafood lunch (try **shirasu-don**, whitebait rice, the local speciality), and sit in a beach café.
- **Sunset with Mt. Fuji over the sea** from Enoshima. It's free, and one of the best views near Tokyo.
- Beers on the beach from the konbini. Drinking outdoors is legal; just take your cans with you.

### Sat 28 · Kamakura temples → Hakone onsen
- Morning:
  - **Great Buddha** (¥300 · ₹180).
  - **Hase-dera** (¥400 · ₹240), with **autumn leaves at their peak** and ocean views.
  - Street food on **Komachi-dori**.
- Around midday, **JR to Odawara → Hakone Tozan line** (~1.5h, ~¥1,500 · ₹920).
- Check into a **hostel with its own onsen** and soak for the evening. This is the most relaxing night of the trip.

### Sun 29 · Hakone morning → Tokyo
- Morning onsen. If it's clear, take a short trip to **Lake Ashi** for Fuji views (or just stay in the bath).
- **Odakyu line to Shinjuku**: ~2h on the local express (~¥1,200 · ₹730), or the **Romancecar** with big windows (~¥2,500 · ₹1,500).
- In Tokyo by mid-afternoon.

# 25–29 Nov · Beach escape from Haneda (budget)

**Land:** Wed 25 Nov, 15:30, Haneda · **Be in Tokyo by:** Sun 29 Nov evening
**Route:** Haneda → **Atami** (1 night) → **Shimoda** (2 nights) → **Kamakura / Enoshima** (1 night) → Tokyo
**Rate:** ¥1 ≈ ₹0.61

**Weather:** about 12–17 °C in the day and 6–10 °C at night. The sea is ~18 °C, so it's a beach for chilling and walking,
not swimming, unless you're brave or in a wetsuit. **The sun sets around 16:30**, so start your days early.
In return, late November has clear skies, **Mt. Fuji views over the sea**, autumn leaves in Kamakura, and empty beaches.

---

## Before you leave Haneda
- **Send your big suitcase ahead.** Use the luggage delivery counter in arrivals (Yamato / JAL ABC, ~¥2,500–3,500 · ₹1,500–2,100)
  and ship it to your Tokyo address for the 29th. Check that the place accepts deliveries. Then you travel with just a backpack.
- Add **Suica** to your phone and top it up. The Izukyu line to Shimoda may need a paper ticket, which you can buy at the station.
- Get cash from a 7-Eleven ATM in the airport.

---

## Day by day

### Wed 25 · Haneda → Atami (onsen by the sea)
- 15:30 land. Immigration and customs take ~1h.
- **Keikyu line to Shinagawa or Yokohama, then JR Tokaido line to Atami.** It's ~2h and ~¥2,300 (₹1,400) in total.
- Arrive ~19:00–19:30. Check into a guesthouse or hostel, soak in an **onsen** (~¥1,000 · ₹610) and have a seafood dinner.

### Thu 26 · Atami → Jogasaki Coast → Shimoda
- Morning walk along **Atami Sun Beach**.
- Take the JR Ito line + Izukyu south along the coast (sit on the **left** side for sea views).
- Stop at **Izu-Kogen** for the **Jogasaki Coast**: volcanic cliffs, a suspension bridge over crashing waves, and a free coastal trail.
- Continue to **Shimoda** (~¥2,000–2,500 · ₹1,200–1,500 for Atami → Shimoda in total). Sunset at the harbour.
- Dinner: **kinmedai** (golden-eye snapper), the local speciality. Look for lunch or teishoku sets to keep it cheap.

### Fri 27 · Shimoda beach day 🏖️
- **Shirahama Ohama Beach** has white sand, turquoise water, and a **shrine torii gate on the rocks** right on the beach.
- **Tatadohama** and **Sotoura** are quieter beaches nearby, good for a book and a konbini beer.
- **Ryugu Cave** is a sea cave with a heart-shaped opening in the rocks, near Tahama.
- Get around by local bus (~¥1,000 · ₹610 for the day) or rent a bicycle.
- Evening: izakaya in Shimoda town.

### Sat 28 · Shimoda → Enoshima (sunset with Fuji)
- Morning swim if you dare, or one last beach walk.
- Izukyu + JR to **Fujisawa** (~3h, ~¥3,500 · ₹2,100), then the **Enoden** retro coastal train (a day pass is ~¥800 · ₹490).
- **Enoshima island:** caves, shrines, and **sunset with Mt. Fuji over the sea**. It's one of the best free views near Tokyo.
- Stay in a hostel in **Kamakura** or Enoshima. Saturday night drinks by the beach at **Yuigahama**.

### Sun 29 · Kamakura → Tokyo
- **Great Buddha** (Kotoku-in, ¥300 · ₹180) and **Hase-dera** (¥400 · ₹240), with ocean views and **autumn leaves at their peak**.
- **Komachi-dori** street food, then a walk on **Yuigahama beach** or **Yuigahama/Zaimokuza** with the surfers.
- **JR to Tokyo** (~1h, ~¥950 · ₹580). Aim to leave by ~15:00 and you'll be in Tokyo by ~16:00–17:00.

**Money savers:**
- Konbini breakfasts, and big lunch sets instead of dinners.
- Konbini beer on the beach. Drinking outdoors is legal in Japan; just take your cans with you.
- Local trains instead of the Odoriko limited express (which saves ~¥2–3k each way).

## Pack
- A light jacket or hoodie for the evenings.
- Sandals and a towel.
- Swimwear, just in case.
- Sunscreen.
- A power bank.

---

**Want to actually swim?** The only warm option on these dates is **Okinawa**: 21–24 °C with a ~24 °C sea. Fly Haneda → Naha
~19:00 on the 25th and back midday on the 29th. It costs about ₹27,000–43,000 with budget flights. Ask me to switch the plan.

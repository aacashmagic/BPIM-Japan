# 25–29 Nov · Relaxed Tokyo (budget)

**Land:** Wed 25 Nov, 15:30, Haneda · **Course starts:** Sun 29 Nov
**Idea:** one base (no repacking), one area per day, slow mornings, evenings out.
**Rate:** ¥1 ≈ ₹0.61 · **Weather:** ~10–16 °C, sunset ~16:30

## Base: Asakusa (4 nights)
- There's a **direct train from Haneda**: the Keikyu line runs through to the Toei Asakusa line in ~45 min for ~¥600 (₹370). No changes, and you can take your suitcase.
- It has the cheapest good hostels in central Tokyo (~¥3,000–4,500 · ₹1,800–2,700/night), and it's calm at night and close to Akihabara and Ueno.
- On the 29th, move to wherever your course is. If the course venue is known, book near it instead.

## Transport
Buy the **Tokyo Subway Ticket (72h, ~¥1,500 · ₹920)** at Haneda arrivals. It gives unlimited Tokyo Metro and Toei subway rides. Use Suica
for everything else (JR, the Yurikamome line to Odaiba, and so on).

## Day by day

### Wed 25 · Arrive → Asakusa evening
- Land 15:30, then take the direct train to Asakusa and check in.
- **Senso-ji temple lit up at night**. It's free, beautiful, and empty after dark.
- **Hoppy Street**: cheap outdoor izakaya, beer, and stewed beef (nikomi).
- Walk to the Sumida River for the **Tokyo Skytree** night view.

### Thu 26 · Anime & electronics: Akihabara + Ueno
- Late start. **Akihabara** (5 min away):
  - Multi-floor arcades (SEGA, GiGO).
  - **Super Potato** for retro games.
  - **Radio Kaikan** for figures and anime goods.
  - **Yodobashi Akiba** for gadgets.
  - Tax-free shopping over ¥5,000 (bring your passport).
- Afternoon: **Ueno Park** autumn leaves, then **Ameyoko** market for street food.
- Evening: standing beer bars and izakaya **under the train tracks at Ameyoko**, one of the cheapest places to drink in Tokyo.

### Fri 27 · Tech + Tokyo's beach: Toyosu & Odaiba
- **teamLab Planets** in Toyosu (~¥4,000 · ₹2,400, **book online ahead**). It's an immersive digital art space where you walk barefoot through water. Tattoos are no problem.
- Ride the driverless **Yurikamome** train over the bay to **Odaiba**:
  - **Life-size Unicorn Gundam** (free) at DiverCity.
  - **Miraikan** future-science museum (~¥630 · ₹380), with robots and humanoids.
  - **Odaiba Seaside Park beach**: real sand and a sunset view of **Rainbow Bridge** and the city skyline. It's not for swimming, but it's great for chilling with a konbini beer.
- Friday night in **Shibuya**:
  - The Scramble crossing (free views from **Shibuya Hikarie**'s 11th floor).
  - **Nonbei Yokocho** tiny bars.
  - Clubs if you're up for it.

### Sat 28 · Chill neighbourhoods: Harajuku → Shimokitazawa → Shinjuku
- Slow morning. **Meiji Jingu** shrine and forest (free), then **Takeshita Street** crepes in Harajuku.
- Afternoon: **Shimokitazawa**, a laid-back area with thrift shops, cafés, records and craft beer.
- Evening in **Shinjuku**:
  - **Tokyo Metropolitan Government Building observatory** (**free** city night view).
  - **Omoide Yokocho** yakitori alley.
  - Saturday drinks in **Golden Gai**'s tiny bars (seat fee ¥500–1,000 · ₹300–610).
- ⚠️ **Kabukicho:** don't follow street touts or "friendly girls" inviting you to a bar. It's a known overcharging scam.

### Sun 29 · Easy morning → course
- Choose one:
  - **Nakano Broadway** (anime and retro collectibles, less touristy than Akihabara).
  - **Kichijoji**: Inokashira Park autumn leaves and rowing boats. The **Ghibli Museum** is here, but tickets go on sale on the 10th of the previous month (**10 Oct for November**) and sell out.
- Check out and head to your course accommodation.

## 🖋️ Tattoos in Tokyo
- All of the above is fine with tattoos.
- For a **hot bath**, Tokyo's neighbourhood **sento** (public baths) are usually tattoo-friendly and cost ~¥550 (₹340). One is **Jakotsuyu in Asakusa**, right by your hostel. Check the current rules before you go.
